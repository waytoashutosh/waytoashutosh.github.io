04 — COMPUTER VISIONIMAGE REGISTRATION

Aligning images for super-resolution

Built a deep image-registration pipeline to align wide-angle and telephoto images for super-resolution training.

Computer visionCNNsSpatial transformers
Research notes +

The problem. Images from different cameras need alignment before they can form useful training pairs.

My contribution. Built preprocessing, training, and evaluation around convolutional features and a Spatial Transformer Network.

Takeaway. Data quality and geometric alignment are as central to an effective learning pipeline as the model architecture.

05 — WHERE IT STARTEDRASPBERRY PI / OPENCV
Illustrative vehicle and track

MNNIT ALLAHABAD · UNDERGRADUATE PROJECT

A small car. A big starting point.

Built a miniature self-driving car that used camera images and a CNN to classify steering directions and drive a physical track.

Imitation learningOpenCV / KerasRaspberry Pi
Build notes +

The system. A Raspberry Pi 3 processed Pi Camera images, with an Arduino UNO handling motor control through serial communication.

The learning task. Trained a CNN with 2,000 images per steering class: left, right, straight, sharp left, and sharp right. Image processing included region masking and Hough transforms.

Why it matters to me. This was an early step toward my interest in robot learning and physical autonomy.
